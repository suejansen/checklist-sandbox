Moving House

3 Months Out

0% complete

  • Consider moving company or truck rental costs, packing supplies, cleaning costs, deposits, temporary accommodation if needed, and a contingency amount.

  • Compare multiple quotes, check reviews, confirm insurance coverage, and check cancellation or rescheduling policies.

  • Decide what to keep, donate, sell, recycle, or dispose of before paying to move it.

2 Months Out

0% complete

  • Confirm the date with your new home, current landlord, employer, and anyone helping with the move.

  • Check your lease requirements and send written notice within the required time frame.

  • Gather sturdy boxes, tape, labels, protective wrap, and any specialty materials you will need.

1 Month Out

0% complete

  • Schedule the shutoff and start dates for electricity, water, gas, internet, and other services.

  • Notify banks, subscriptions, insurance providers, government services, and important contacts.

  • Request enough time away from work for loading, travel, unloading, and settling in.

2 Weeks Out

0% complete

  • Pack seasonal items, spare linens, books, decorations, and other things you can live without for now.

  • Plan safe care for moving day so children and pets are away from busy doorways and vehicles.

  • Reconfirm arrival time, addresses, access instructions, contact details, and the final estimate.

Moving Week

0% complete

  • Set aside toiletries, medication, chargers, clothes, documents, basic tools, and anything needed for the first night.

  • Finish cleaning, remove rubbish, and check cupboards, storage spaces, and outdoor areas for anything left behind.

  • Make sure you know when and where to collect the new keys and how movers will access both properties.

Moving Day

0% complete

  • Carry valuables, identification, medication, keys, and essential documents rather than placing them on the moving truck.

  • Check every room, cupboard, loft, garage, and outdoor space before locking up and handing over the keys.

  • Note any existing damage, confirm utilities are working, and decide where large furniture should go.

After the Move

0% complete

  • Set up your bed, bathroom, kitchen basics, and a comfortable place to rest before tackling every box.

  • Record meter readings, update delivery instructions, and save important warranty and service information.

  • Break down boxes, reuse what you can, and recycle or dispose of packing materials responsibly.
